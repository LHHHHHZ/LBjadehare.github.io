Set Transformer Guided NSGA-III for Large-Scale Multi-Objective Optimization

Huazhong Lin1 · Qin'an Zheng1 · Pengfei Zhou1 · Yu Xiang1 · Yichu Liu1 · Chengwang Xie1✉ · Fahong Yu2

1School of Data Science and Engineering, South China Normal University, China 2Center of Intelligent Computing and Security Research, Shanwei Institute of Technology, China

chengwangxie@m.scnu.edu.cn

Abstract. Large-scale multiobjective optimization problems (LSMOPs) typically involve hundreds or even thousands of decision variables, and their "curse of dimensionality" poses a severe challenge to traditional multiobjective evolutionary algorithms (MOEAs), significantly hindering both convergence and diversity of the solution set. To address this challenge, this paper proposes a novel hybrid framework—Set Transformer guided NSGA-III (SET-NSGAIII)—which integrates the permutation invariance and global context-aware modeling capabilities of the Set Transformer into the population iteration process of NSGA-III. The Set Transformer module treats the entire population at the current generation as an unordered set and learns a compact representation, thereby enabling a more thorough consideration of the complex inter-variable dependencies commonly present in LSMOPs. Furthermore, a dynamically adjusted component is incorporated into the model's loss function to guide the generation of higher-quality offspring. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm consistently and significantly outperforms or is on par with state-of-the-art LSMOP algorithms in terms of the IGDp performance metric.

Keywords: Large-Scale Multi-Objective Optimization, Transformer, Evolutionary Computation, Neural Networks.
